# Phase 17.1 Math Reference

## Model-Free Expected Move

For the front expiry, expected move uses CBOE-style model-free variance replication over the listed smile:

\[
\sigma^2 = \frac{2}{T}\sum_i \frac{\Delta K_i}{K_i^2} Q(K_i) - \frac{1}{T}\left(\frac{F}{K_0}-1\right)^2
\]

Where:
- \(K_i\): strike grid points
- \(\Delta K_i\): strike spacing weight around each strike
- \(Q(K_i)\): option mid-price at strike \(K_i\) (invalid/zero mids excluded)
- \(F\): forward price estimate
- \(K_0\): first strike at or below forward
- \(T\): time to expiry in years

Converted to a front-expiry percentage move:

\[
\text{Expected Move \%} = \sqrt{\sigma^2 T} \times 100
\]

Card display uses: `±X.X% by MMM DD (Nd)`.

## 25Δ Risk Reversal

Risk reversal is the directional skew spread at 25-delta:

\[
\text{RR}_{25\Delta} = IV_{put,25\Delta} - IV_{call,25\Delta}
\]

Interpretation:
- Higher positive RR: downside protection more bid than upside calls
- Lower/negative RR: comparatively flatter or upside-tilted skew

## 25Δ Butterfly

Butterfly measures smile convexity at 25-delta around ATM:

\[
\text{Fly}_{25\Delta} = 0.5\,(IV_{put,25\Delta} + IV_{call,25\Delta}) - IV_{ATM}
\]

Interpretation:
- Higher Fly: stronger tail-demand convexity versus ATM
- Lower Fly: flatter wings versus ATM

## Straddle Approximation (Reference Only)

A common shortcut is:

\[
\text{Approx EM} \approx IV_{ATM}\sqrt{T}
\]

This is useful for intuition, but the locked implementation does **not** use this approximation for Phase 17.1 outputs. The runtime path uses model-free integration to capture full-smile and tail information instead of flat-vol assumptions.
