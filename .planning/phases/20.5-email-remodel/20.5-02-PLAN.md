<objective>
Bump the resolution of every kaleido-exported PNG chart attachment (D-06) so they stay legible when a PM zooms in on a phone mail client, without changing the charts' on-screen display width. This is a config-driven visual constant, not a magic number, matching the existing KALEIDO_CAMERA_EYE convention in engine/config.py.

Purpose: One of the two visual-fidelity levers in the email remodel (the other is the mobile-safe HTML width in Plan 03) -- both fixed-width mail layout and zoomed-in image legibility need separate handling because a mail client can be pinch-zoomed independent of the declared HTML width.
Output: engine/config.py gains KALEIDO_SCALE_FACTOR; engine/report/png_export.py applies it to every write_image() call.
</objective>
