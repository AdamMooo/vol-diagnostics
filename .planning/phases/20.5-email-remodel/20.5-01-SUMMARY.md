## Accomplishments
- `load_chain()` now captures `fetched_at = datetime.datetime.now(ET)` at the exact point the CBOE JSON response is received — a genuine wall-clock fact, not a fabrication of `as_of`'s date-only granularity.
- `load_chain()` computes `filter_drop_pct`, the OI-weighted percentage of the raw (non-0DTE) chain dropped by the `min_oi`/`iv`/`max_iv` quality filters, with 0DTE contracts correctly excluded from both the numerator and denominator.
- `compute_ticker()`'s summary dict now exposes `as_of`, `fetched_at`, and `filter_drop_pct`, read directly off the snapshot — Plan 03's report.py can format these without touching the network or recomputing anything.

## Decisions Made
- Kept `as_of` (a `datetime.date`) untouched — it still drives DTE/T_years elsewhere in the pipeline. `fetched_at` is additive, not a replacement.
- Accumulators (`raw_oi_total`, `kept_oi_total`) live inside the existing `for opt in data["options"]:` loop, incremented at the exact points specified in the plan (`raw_oi_total` right after the `dte < min_dte` continue, `kept_oi_total` right after the quality-filter continue), so the scoping to non-0DTE contracts falls out naturally from existing control flow rather than a second pass over the data.
