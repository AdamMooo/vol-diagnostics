<objective>
Rebuild the daily email's header and footer chrome around the two ship-blocker gaps identified in the 2026-05-11 pre-distribution audit and folded into this phase: a real snapshot-freshness timestamp, and a promoted, visually distinct methodology caveat about GEX magnitude. Also apply the mobile-safe fixed width (D-05) and surface the new filter-drop-ratio disclosure (D-07) in the existing two-tier "Quick assumptions / Deep methodology" footer.

This plan is entirely additive/in-place editing of `engine/report/report.py` -- no new file, no section reordering (D-03 locks the current Evolution -> Cards -> failed-note -> PNG-note -> footer order). It depends on Plan 01 (`20.5-01`) for the new `as_of`/`fetched_at`/`filter_drop_pct` keys on the summary dict -- this plan only formats those values, it never recomputes or re-fetches them (report.py stays I/O-free per the project's compute-once-thread-through rule).

**D-01 placement decision:** both new header elements go directly below the existing `_section_header(...)` title/date line, above the Evolution section -- this is the cleanest "header block" reading, and keeps the two new elements as a fixed prelude rather than tucking them below content that may itself be cold-start-omitted (Evolution is sometimes absent entirely).

**D-02 visual distinction decision:** the timestamp line is a plain, small, gray text line (matches the existing `png_note`/`_kv_cell` label-gray convention -- a fact, not a warning). The caveat banner reuses the file's other established "framed block" vocabulary -- a left accent bar plus a light tinted background (`background:#f1f5f9`, readable in both light and typical dark-mode override behavior), distinct from the plain timestamp line and from the plain bordered `methodology_footer`.

Purpose: Close two real, previously-shipped-with gaps (never-implemented freshness disclosure, buried methodology caveat) while keeping the email/dashboard canonical-card parity seam intact -- none of this is routed through `card_model.py` because it is email chrome, not a card field.
Output: Two new header elements in every email; footer gains one new disclosure bullet and loses one now-redundant bullet; body width shrunk for mobile.
</objective>
