<objective>
Fix three methodology defects in the calibration replay that produced Phase 26's shipped alert bands (90/94/85/5): WR-01 (episodes/week denominator pools sessions across concurrent metrics, understating the monitor-wide rate ~5x), WR-02 (flicker_ratio mixes calendar days with a session-denominated window and ignores escalations), and WR-03 (replay excludes today from the ECDF window while production includes it — a train/serve skew). Also fix WR-07 (calibration test hits live yfinance/parquet stores). Then re-run calibration with the corrected methodology and update `config.py`'s `MONITOR_ALERT_BAND_*`/`MONITOR_ALERT_HYSTERESIS_GAP` constants with the actual new evidence — whatever the corrected replay recommends, not necessarily the same 90/94/85/5 that shipped from the biased methodology.

Purpose: The bands are only as trustworthy as the replay that calibrated them (D-05/D-06). This plan depends on Plan 26-04 landing first (both touch `engine/config.py`, and the corrected-pipeline sequencing the gap-closure scope calls for: land independent correctness fixes, then re-run calibration against the corrected code).
Output: Corrected `engine/monitor/calibration.py`, a hermetic `test_calibration.py`, and `engine/config.py` band constants re-derived from an actual corrected calibration run.
</objective>
