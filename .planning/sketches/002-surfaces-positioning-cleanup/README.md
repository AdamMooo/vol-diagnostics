# Sketch 002: Surfaces & Positioning Cleanup

## Design Question
Once page-1 has the environment read + key levels, what do the secondary pages look like?

## How to View
Open `.planning/sketches/002-surfaces-positioning-cleanup/index.html` in a browser.

## Variants
- **A: Trend-First Surfaces** — The evolution/momentum story IS the page hero. Shows 2×2 trend cards (level/rms/skew/term with 5d deltas), a multi-horizon momentum strip (1d/5d/10d/20d), then 3D surface and video below. The question "where is vol heading?" is answered before you ever see the surface.
- **B: Minimal Positioning** — Since key levels are already on page 1, the positioning page is just the deep-dive evidence: clean OI table + gamma profile model derivation. No redundancy.

## What to Look For
- A: Does the momentum strip (1d→5d→10d→20d) showing direction per horizon give you what you meant by "gradients"?
- A: Are the 2×2 trend cards useful or would you prefer fewer?
- B: Is this positioning page enough or too stripped back?
- Overall: Ticker pills (SPY/QQQ/IWM) vs radio buttons — which feels cleaner?
