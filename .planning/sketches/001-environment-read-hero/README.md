# Sketch 001: Environment Read Hero

## Design Question
When you open the dashboard, what's the first thing you see? Three approaches to answering "what's the environment right now?"

## How to View
Open `.planning/sketches/001-environment-read-hero/index.html` in a browser.

## Variants
- **A: Morning Brief** — One bold editorial paragraph that synthesizes all the signals into a narrative, with 3 compact cards below as evidence. The paragraph IS the product; cards are footnotes.
- **B: Signal Board** — Colored risk bar (ELEVATED/NORMAL) at top, 8-metric grid with context lines, and a "what it means" block below. Data-first, read-second.
- **C: Conversation** — Huge type, two paragraphs that tell you everything in plain English. Evidence cells at the bottom are minimal (one line per ticker). Maximum readability, minimum chrome.

## What to Look For
- Which variant answers your question fastest (10 seconds or less)?
- Is the narrative (A/C) or the data grid (B) more useful for your morning flow?
- Does the risk bar in B feel gimmicky or genuinely useful?
- Is C too sparse — do you want the numbers there or is the narrative enough?
- Font sizes: is A/C readable at a glance or still too small?

## Realistic Data Used
All numbers match the current state reported when you launched the dashboard:
- 0/3 dealers stabilizing (all amplifying)
- 2/3 VRP rich (SPY + QQQ above realized)
- Avg IV30: 22.1%
- SPY front skew: 87th percentile
