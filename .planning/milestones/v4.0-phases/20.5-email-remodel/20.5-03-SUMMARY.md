# Phase 20.5 Plan 03: Email Header/Footer Chrome Rebuild Summary

**Daily email gains a real snapshot-freshness timestamp and a visually distinct, always-present GEX-magnitude methodology banner below the title; body shrinks to a mobile-safe 390px; footer discloses per-ticker filter-drop ratio and drops the now-redundant duplicate bullet.**

## Performance

- **Duration:** ~15 min
- **Completed:** 2026-07-16
- **Tasks:** 2/2 completed
- **Files modified:** 2

## Accomplishments

- `_snapshot_timestamp_line(r)` reads `r["fetched_at"]` (threaded through by Plan 01) and renders `"Snapshot {date} {time} ET · OI T-1 · Greeks 15-min delayed"` as a plain gray fact line, or omits entirely when unavailable — no placeholder text ever shown.
- `_methodology_caveat_banner()` is a static, non-data-derived, always-present framed block (light tint + left accent bar) carrying the "Sign & order of magnitude are load-bearing" caveat, promoted out of the buried footer into a prominent header position.
- Both new elements are inserted directly below `_section_header(...)`, above the Evolution section — a fixed prelude that survives even when Evolution itself is cold-start-omitted.
- Email body `max-width` shrunk from 720px to 390px (mobile-safe), with no `@media` queries added (Outlook-COM-safe, inline-style-only constraint preserved).
- `methodology_footer` gains a per-ticker filter-drop-pct bullet (D-07), built from `index_results`, omitted entirely when no ticker has a `filter_drop_pct` value (cold-start-safe — `None` never renders as `"—%"`/`"NaN%"`).
- The GEX-magnitude caveat bullet was removed from the footer's Deep-methodology tier since its content now lives exclusively in the new banner — verified no duplication (`grep -c` returns exactly 1 across the whole file).

## Decisions Made

- Both header elements sit as a fixed prelude above `evol_html` rather than below it, so they are never accidentally hidden behind a cold-start-omitted Evolution section.
- The caveat banner deliberately departs from the module's "no card backgrounds" principle (documented in the file's own docstring) — this is an intentional, D-02-mandated exception scoped to exactly one element.
- `_fmt_pct` was imported twice under two aliases (`_fmt_signed_pct`, `_fmt_unsigned_pct`) rather than adding a new formatter function, since the existing call sites always use the default `signed=True` and this plan's one `signed=False` call needed a distinct, self-documenting name.
