<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Add two new descriptive reads to the per-ticker card (dashboard + daily email via
the PAR-01 canonical seam) for SPY/QQQ/IWM:

1. **Front-expiry model-free expected move** — ±% range over the front expiry's

   "1d σ" suffix in the IV30 card field.

2. **25Δ butterfly** (smile convexity) — ½(25Δput_iv + 25Δcall_iv) − ATM_iv for

Deliverable also includes a **math reference document** (`17.1-MATH.md`) explaining
all formulas used: VIX model-free variance, butterfly definition, 25Δ conventions,
and the straddle approximation for reference.

Raw labeled numbers only. No scoring, no categorical labels, no trade prescription.

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Expected Move — VIX Model-Free Variance

- **D-01:** The expected move is computed via the **CBOE VIX methodology** (model-free
  variance), NOT the ATM_IV × √(T) approximation and NOT BS straddle pricing.
  Formula: `σ² = (2/T) × Σᵢ [ΔKᵢ/Kᵢ²] × Midᵢ − (1/T)(F/K₀ − 1)²`
  where Kᵢ are strike levels, Midᵢ are option mid-prices (½(bid+ask)), F is the
  forward price (from ATM call-put parity or spot), K₀ is the first strike ≤ F.
  Taking √(σ² × T) gives the ±% expected move over DTE days. This integrates the
  full smile and captures tail risk that flat-vol BS misses.

- **D-02:** Computation is isolated in a new helper
  `engine/vol/vol_metrics.py:compute_model_free_em(df, spot, front_expiry)` — pure
  function, no I/O, takes the chain df filtered to the front expiry.

- **D-03:** The result replaces the `±X.XX%` suffix in the existing `IV30 / 1d σ`
  card field. The field label becomes `IV30 / EM` and the value reads:
  `"18.5% (+0.8) · ±2.1% by Jun 27 (5d)"` — IV30 with delta, then expected move
  with explicit expiry date and DTE.

- **D-04:** When no bid/ask prices are available for a strike (zero or NaN mid),
  that strike is excluded from the sum — standard CBOE practice. If fewer than
  3 valid strikes exist for the front expiry, EM is None and the suffix shows "—".

### Butterfly — ATM Anchor & Computation

- **D-05:** Extend `compute_skew_25d()` to return `atm_iv` per bucket alongside
  the existing `put_iv`, `call_iv`, `skew`, `dte`. Within the same expiry group
  already being processed, find the nearest OTM call to spot as ATM proxy (same
  logic as `compute_term_structure()`'s ATM selection). This ensures the butterfly's
  ATM anchor is from the exact same expiry as the 25Δ options — no cross-expiry
  mismatch possible.

- **D-06:** Butterfly is computed in `compute_ticker()` from the extended skew dict:
  `butterfly = 0.5 * (front_month.put_iv + front_month.call_iv) - front_month.atm_iv`
  Result is stored as `butterfly` in the summary dict passed to `build_card_fields()`.
  Precomputed in the engine layer — card builder remains I/O-free.

### Butterfly History & Percentile

- **D-07:** Add a `butterfly` float column to `out/gex_snapshots.parquet`. Written
  by `run_daily` alongside `front_skew`. Old snapshots load safely (new column
  defaults to NaN). No new store file needed.

- **D-08:** Percentile uses `scipy.stats.percentileofscore(kind="rank")` over the
  stored butterfly history — identical pattern to VRP percentile (Phase 16) and
  skew percentile. Lookback = `VRP_PERCENTILE_LOOKBACK` (252) from config.

- **D-09:** Cold-start gating mirrors Phase 16: when fewer than the lookback sessions
  exist, label the actual available count (e.g. "74th %ile · 42 sessions (building
  to 252)"). Below a minimum threshold (to be determined by planner — Phase 16 used
  ~10 as the floor), omit the percentile entirely rather than compute on a trivially
  thin sample.

### Card Placement & Labeling

- **D-10:** Card field order (updated section):
  - `IV30 / EM` — IV30 with delta suffix + model-free expected move `±X.X% by MMM DD (Nd)`
  - `Skew (25Δ)` — existing risk reversal (put_iv − call_iv) with delta suffix
  - `25Δ Fly` — butterfly with percentile context: `+0.8pp · 42nd %ile`
  - VRP and walls unchanged below

- **D-11:** Expected move label format: `±X.X% by MMM DD (Nd)` — e.g. `±2.1% by Jun 27 (5d)`.
  Expiry date formatted as `%b %d` (no year — always near-term). DTE is integer days.

- **D-12:** Butterfly field label: `25Δ Fly`. Value format: `+0.8pp · 74th %ile`
  (or `+0.8pp · 42 sessions` during cold-start, or `+0.8pp` alone if below minimum
  threshold).

### Math Reference Document

- **D-13:** Deliver `17.1-MATH.md` in the phase directory as a plain-English + formula
  explanation of: (1) VIX model-free variance derivation, (2) 25Δ risk reversal
  definition, (3) 25Δ butterfly definition and what it measures (smile convexity /
  tail demand vs direction), (4) the IV30/√252 1-day sigma approximation for reference.
  This document is for the user's learning — not consumed by downstream agents.

</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Model-free EM formula (CBOE VIX method, single-expiry):
  `σ² = (2/T) × Σᵢ [ΔKᵢ/Kᵢ²] × Midᵢ − (1/T)(F/K₀ − 1)²`
  Output: `em_pct = √(σ² × T) × 100` gives ±% move over DTE horizon.
  Strikes with zero or NaN mid are excluded. Minimum 3 valid strikes required.

- Butterfly formula: `fly = 0.5 × (put_iv + call_iv) − atm_iv` (all in pp).
  Positive = smile has convexity (tails bid up); negative = unusual, tails cheap.

- Card field examples:
  - `IV30 / EM: 18.5% (+0.8) · ±2.1% by Jun 27 (5d)`
  - `25Δ Fly: +0.8pp · 74th %ile` (or `+0.8pp · 38 sessions` cold-start)

- Math doc (`17.1-MATH.md`) covers: VIX methodology derivation (why model-free beats
  flat-vol), 25Δ conventions (what delta means for OTM options), RR vs fly (direction
  vs convexity), the log-contract replication argument behind VIX, and the simple
  ATM_IV × √(T) approximation for comparison.

</specifics>
